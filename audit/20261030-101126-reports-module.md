## Summary

`/admin/reports` is now fully server-driven:

- **Filters** — From/To dates (defaults to the current month), location and category; Apply and Reset. The PDF uses the applied filters.
- **On screen** — total kg, records, average per record, collection count, waste-by-category share bars, collection status breakdown, and waste-by-location table, plus who generated it and when.
- **PDF** — `GET /api/reports/waste-summary/pdf` renders the same report with DomPDF and downloads `ecotrack-report-<from>-to-<to>.pdf`; it includes the filters, totals, tables and generated-by footer.
- **Roles** — admin sees all locations; office users are forced to their office's location (location field is read-only) and only receive that location in `/reports/options`; maintenance is blocked.

## Notes

- Office filtering is by the office's `location_id` because `waste_records` has no `office_id`; adding an office dimension would require a schema change.
