## Summary

The login page now matches the landing page and Fieldwork design system:

- **Layout** — a centered bordered card split into a deep-pine brand panel (desktop) and the sign-in form; on mobile the panel becomes a compact dark brand header above the form.
- **Brand panel** — EcoTrack lockup, "Every collection accounted for.", a "Today's route" progress card (7/12, Collected/Scheduled/Missed) and an audit-log note. No gradients or glass.
- **Form** — email-or-username + password, 44px inputs, show/hide password, inline `role="alert"` error, 44px submit button with loading state.
- **Extras** — "Back to home" link to `/`, demo-account chips (Administrator / Maintenance / Office) that fill the seed credentials, and the JWT/security note.
- **Auth flow unchanged** — success uses the backend `redirect`; `PublicOnly` still sends logged-in users away from `/login`.
